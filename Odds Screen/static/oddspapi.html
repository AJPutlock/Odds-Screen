Prices come from OddsPapi's free historical-odds endpoint: its last entry per line is the current price. One call per game per 3 books with a 5 s cooldown, so 6 books on a 14-game NFL slate takes about 2½ min. The game list costs 1 of the 250 monthly requests and is cached 6 h.
